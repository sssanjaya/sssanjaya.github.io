Handover in Ten

Turn a messy day of half-finished work into a note someone else can pick up cold, without calling you.

Fixes Handovers that say “mostly done, ping me” and leave the next person guessing.
How It asks one question at a time, starting from what is still broken, not from what you did.
You get A one-page note: current state, next three actions, traps, and who to ask.

Try it with

“I am out from tomorrow for a week. The billing export job is half migrated: the new script runs but skips refunds, the old cron is still on, and only I know why the staging data looks wrong.”

How it runs

1 What is still broken. The state right now, not the history.
2 What only you know. The odd facts that live in your head.
3 The next three actions. In order, each one checkable.
4 The cold-read test. It rereads the note as a stranger and asks what is unclear.
